Undergraduate research · DREAM Lab

When can you trust a fast approximation?

I study "package queries" — database questions whose exact answers require solving hard integer programs. 120 controlled experiments across 5 query templates measure exactly when a fast LP relaxation gives the right answer (82% overall, 100% on count-only queries) and when it quietly fails.
